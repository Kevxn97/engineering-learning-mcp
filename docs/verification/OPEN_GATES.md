# Open gates — no shared/production approval

- Real Claude Code, Codex and Cursor versions, actual callbacks, refresh behavior,
  loaded instruction files and cross-client workflow: **NOT VERIFIED**.
- Company issuer claim mapping, native client registration, group/membership ownership,
  network routing and provider/data-processing approval: **NOT VERIFIED**.
- Production TLS, proxy abuse controls, secret management, storage/backup encryption,
  approved retention and operational owner: **NOT VERIFIED**.
- Physical database backup/restore drill and post-backup external retirement register
  freshness: **NOT VERIFIED**. Automated logical replay is narrower evidence.
- Sustained 10 requests/second with 10,000 entries on the constrained local reference:
  **TARGET NOT MET**. Authorization lookup was optimized; the maintained scanner's
  per-process startup and bounded backpressure still limit capacity. Do not disable
  scanning or silently cache confidential content to make the benchmark green.
- Real business usefulness versus a maintained static runbook: **NOT VERIFIED**.
- SDK-only tests do not certify IDEs, human review does not prove truth, and a green
  correctness job is not all of the above. Native/cloud acceptance stays separate.

Known MVP limitations: ordinary UI forms expose advanced edits as structured JSON;
project-to-team scope expansion is deliberately manual; team revisions can be
replaced by a new reviewed derivation rather than a native agent widening audience;
SCM permissions and test evidence are not fetched/synchronized; feedback on an already
unreadable revision must use the human incident process; bounded search considers at
most 100 eligible text candidates; no global private content cache or bulk export.
