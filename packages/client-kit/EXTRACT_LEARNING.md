# Task-end extraction instruction

Review the completed task for zero to three reusable observations. Do not invent
observations, tests, causes or failed attempts. Distinguish facts from hypotheses.

Only propose material that is non-trivial, not already adequately documented,
observed in a concrete case, bounded by explicit applicability and limitations,
and independently evidenced. Failed or inconclusive experiments may qualify when
honestly labeled. Do not generalize one incident to “always” or “never”. Remove
secrets and personal/customer data; do not copy complete source files or sessions.

For each candidate prepare the strict LearningBody defined by the service. Before
transmission apply the shared rules: permission, participation, developer consent
or previously enabled auto-propose, duplicate search and stable UUID idempotency.
A proposal is not published knowledge. Zero candidates is an acceptable outcome.
